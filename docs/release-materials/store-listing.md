# DrawDraw store listing — draft

Release scope: free drawing tools, with Pro clearly unavailable. This is the prepared default while the billing question remains unanswered; no paid billing provider has been selected.

Name: **DrawDraw: Portrait Guides**

Short description: **Fit a 3D construction head to portraits and export guides for drawing.**

Description:

DrawDraw helps you study portraits using a construction head that turns with the pose. Tap the chin, the base of the nose, and the brow to fit the guide, then adjust its angle, position, and proportions.

- Rotate the guide, pinch to scale, and use Move mode to place it.
- Practice from the guide alone, with a quick peek at your reference.
- Export a portrait with its guide, a transparent guide, or a faded tracing layer.
- Reopen saved portraits and their guide settings on your device.
- Use the free tools without ads or watermarks.

Pro features are shown for reference but purchases are unavailable in this release.
