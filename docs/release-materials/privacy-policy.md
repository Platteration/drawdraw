## Portraits and drawing projects

DrawDraw processes portraits and drawing guides on your device. Choosing a portrait or taking a photo creates a copy in the app's local storage. The app keeps that copy and your guide settings so you can reopen the drawing. DrawDraw does not upload these portraits or settings to a publisher-operated server.

You can remove a drawing by holding its thumbnail and selecting Remove. This removes its active project and copied portrait. It does not remove your original source photo or an image previously exported to your photo library or another app.

If a project index becomes partly unreadable, DrawDraw can show the readable drawings and offers recovery. Recovery first preserves a local copy of the original index. Recovery copies can retain drawing settings and image references after an active drawing is removed. Use **Remove recovery copies** on the home screen to delete these snapshots when you no longer need them. This does not remove your current drawings.

If guide settings are damaged, the portrait stays available with default guides and a recovery warning. Recovery keeps the original index before writing the repaired version. Recovery snapshots do not contain independent portrait files and cannot restore previously overwritten or deleted images.

## Camera and photo access

Camera permission is requested when you choose Take a photo. Portrait import uses the operating system's photo picker to access the selected image. Saving an export requests permission to add an image to your photo library where the operating system requires it. The current app does not request microphone access or access to audio/video libraries.

Temporary export images are released after saving or canceling the export choice, and after the iOS sharing activity returns. On Android, the sharing API does not confirm when the receiving app has finished reading, so shared captures remain available in app cache for the capture library or operating system to clean up. Temporary or partial files may also remain if the operating system or storage fails; the app reports supported import cleanup failures.

## Sharing and device backups

Sharing an export sends it to the app or service you select through the system share sheet. The recipient's privacy practices govern its copy. Saving to Photos may also make the image available to any photo-cloud service you have enabled.

Your operating system may include DrawDraw's local data in device backups, depending on your backup settings. Device backups and restore services are controlled by the operating system provider. Removing app data does not necessarily delete an existing device backup or copies held by recipients.

## Accounts, advertising, and purchases

The current build has no DrawDraw account, advertising integration, or app analytics SDK. Pro purchases are unavailable in this build. If purchases are added, the stores and any billing provider will process transaction information, and this policy will explain that processing before the paid feature is released.

## Your choices

You can decline camera or photo-saving permission, remove active drawings, remove recovery copies, delete exported images through their destination apps, and clear DrawDraw's local data through your device settings or by removing the app. Manage cloud backups and shared copies separately through their providers.
